Privacy Policy

Archery Target

Archery Target is a simple single-player VR archery simulation for Meta Quest VR headsets and controllers. This policy explains what information the game accesses and how it uses that information for online leaderboards.

Last updated: November 14, 2026

Quick Summary

  • Online leaderboards use your signed-in Meta Horizon account.
  • The game accesses your Meta Horizon User ID, User Name (username), and User Profile information for the leaderboards.
  • Other players can see your username, score, and rank on the leaderboards.
  • Meta provides your account age group to determine leaderboard eligibility.
  • Request deletion of your data at no charge.
  • No multiplayer features are included.
  • No microphone, voice chat, or camera access is used.
  • Caffeine Studios does not sell personal information or use leaderboard data for advertising.

1. Who We Are

Archery Target is developed and published by Caffeine Studios. The game is a VR archery simulation with three play modes: Time Mode, Perfect Ten, and Infinity Mode.

2. Information We Collect

Archery Target uses Meta Platform services for its online leaderboards. For eligible accounts, the game reads your Meta Horizon User ID to associate scores with your account. It uses Meta Horizon usernames from User Profile information returned by the leaderboard service to display players’ entries. A separate Caffeine Studios account is not required.

The game also receives your Meta-provided account age group to determine whether online leaderboards are available to you. The age group is a broad category. It is not displayed on the leaderboards.

3. Online Leaderboard Data

The game submits eligible scores to Meta Leaderboards for Time Mode and Perfect Ten. Your User ID links your score to your Meta Horizon account. The game retrieves scores and ranks from Meta and displays them alongside the players' usernames. Other players can see your username, score, and rank when your entry appears on a leaderboard.

The game may temporarily store a pending score on your device using your User ID so it can retry submission. It removes that pending score after a successful submission. Meta stores online leaderboard entries. See Request Data Deletion for instructions on requesting removal of your entries.

4. Device Access and Permissions

Archery Target uses standard Meta Quest headset and controller input so the player can aim, shoot, and interact with the VR archery gameplay. The game does not request microphone access, voice chat access, camera access, contacts, precise location, or multiplayer communication features.

Any platform-level processing required for the Meta Quest headset, controllers, store, operating system, or device runtime is handled by Meta under Meta's own terms and privacy practices.

5. How We Use Information

Archery Target uses leaderboard data to submit, retrieve, and display scores and ranks. If a player reports a leaderboard entry, the game uses the reported player's User ID to open Meta's reporting tool.

Caffeine Studios does not use leaderboard data for analytics, advertising, profiling, remarketing, or user tracking.

6. Sharing Information

Meta processes leaderboard information to provide the online leaderboards. Your username, score, and rank may be visible to other Archery Target players. Caffeine Studios does not sell, rent, or trade your personal information.

7. Children's Privacy

Archery Target checks the age group provided by Meta before enabling the online leaderboards. The game enables online leaderboard access for accounts Meta identifies as teen or adult. For accounts Meta identifies as child accounts, the game does not request the User Profile or submit scores to the online leaderboards.

8. Request Data Deletion

You can request deletion of your Archery Target data free of charge from any country. Email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com with the subject Archery Target Data Deletion Request. Include your Meta Horizon username and describe what you want deleted, such as your Time Mode and Perfect Ten leaderboard entries or information you previously sent us. Keep your password and identity documents private.

We may ask for additional details to verify your request. For verified requests, we use Meta’s leaderboard tools to remove the requested entries and delete other requested information we control. If a legal obligation requires us to retain any information, we will explain what we must keep and why.

To remove locally stored scores and pending submissions, clear Archery Target’s app data on your headset. This also clears local progress and settings. For deletion of your Meta account or profile itself, contact Meta.

9. Contacting Us

If a player contacts Caffeine Studios outside the game for support, questions, or business communication, any information provided in that message will be used only to respond to the request.

10. Changes to This Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if Archery Target changes, if new features are added, or if legal or platform requirements change. Updates will be posted on this page with a revised "Last updated" date.

11. Contact

For privacy questions about Archery Target, contact Caffeine Studios at thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com.
