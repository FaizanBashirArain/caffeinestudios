Privacy Policy

Wanted Streets: Open World Crime

Wanted Streets: Open World Crime is an upcoming open world VR action game developed by Caffeine Studios for Meta Quest. This policy describes the planned local-only game data practices for its Coming Soon listing, including saved progress, settings, VR input and support enquiries. We will update this page before introducing features that change these practices.

Last updated: October 14, 2026

Quick Summary

  • Game progress and settings are saved locally on your device.
  • Headset and controller input supports VR gameplay.
  • No Meta profile lookup, online leaderboard, multiplayer, advertising or gameplay analytics is planned.
  • Caffeine Studios does not receive a server-side copy of your game saves.
  • You can remove local saves through app-data controls and contact us about support information.
  • We do not sell player personal information.

1. Who We Are

Caffeine Studios develops Wanted Streets: Open World Crime. For privacy questions, contact thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com. No separate Caffeine Studios account is planned for the game.

2. Local Game Progress and Settings

The game is planned to save gameplay progress and preferences locally on your headset. Depending on the final save system, these records may include mission progress, earned in-game money, vehicle or weapon progress, and control, comfort or audio settings. These records are used to restore your game between sessions. In-game money is a gameplay record, not your bank or payment information. We do not receive a server-side copy of these saves.

3. Headset and Controller Input

The game processes headset and controller position, orientation, button and other controller input to support looking around, movement, driving, aiming, weapon use and menus. This input is used on your device for gameplay. Caffeine Studios does not collect movement histories or use this input for advertising or identification outside the game.

4. Meta Account and Online Features

The planned game does not retrieve your Meta username, display name, user ID, age group or avatar through profile features. No online leaderboards, multiplayer, advertising SDKs, integrated gameplay analytics or Caffeine Studios cloud-save service are planned. The game does not ask you to provide your name, email, contacts, date of birth, precise geographic location or payment details to play.

5. Platform Services

Meta provides the headset operating system, account, store and associated platform services. Meta may process technical, account, purchase or diagnostic information independently under Meta's Privacy Policy and your platform settings. Platform backup features, if enabled, may store or restore app data under Meta's controls. This policy describes Caffeine Studios' game practices and does not replace Meta's policy.

6. Support Enquiries

If you email us, we receive your email address and the information you choose to provide, such as a description of a problem or an attached screenshot. We use it to respond, troubleshoot and handle privacy requests. Please send only what is needed and avoid including passwords, payment details or sensitive information. Contacting support is optional and is separate from locally processed gameplay data.

7. Use and Sharing of Information

Local game data is used to provide gameplay, save progress and restore settings. Support information is used to address your request. We do not sell, rent or trade player personal information, or share local saves with advertisers. Our email service provider processes support correspondence to deliver and store messages. We may disclose information we hold where required by law or necessary to protect users, our rights or the service.

8. Retention and Security

Local saves remain on your device until replaced or removed through the available app-data controls or uninstall process. Platform backups may remain separately and must be managed through the provider's settings. Support correspondence is kept only as long as needed to resolve the request and meet applicable legal obligations. We use reasonable measures appropriate to information under our control; no storage or transmission method guarantees absolute security.

9. Deletion and Your Privacy Choices

You can remove local progress and settings through the headset's app-data controls, where available, or by uninstalling the game. This may permanently reset progress. Manage any platform backup copies separately. Caffeine Studios cannot remotely retrieve or delete a save held only on your headset.

To request access, correction or deletion of information held by Caffeine Studios, including support correspondence, email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com with the subject Wanted Streets Data Deletion Request. Use the email address involved in the correspondence and describe what you want removed. Do not send a password or identity document. We may ask for proportionate information to verify the request. We will respond and address information under our control in accordance with applicable law, and explain any legal limitation. Requests about Meta-controlled account or platform information should be directed to Meta.

Depending on your location, you may also have rights to obtain a copy of your information, object to or restrict processing, or contact your local data protection authority.

10. Children's Privacy

Players must follow the game's published age rating and Meta account requirements. The game is not designed to solicit children's personal information. Parents or guardians may contact us about information a child has provided through support. If we learn that information under our control was collected contrary to applicable children's privacy requirements, we will take appropriate steps to address it.

11. Changes to This Policy

We will revise this policy if the game's information practices change, including before introducing account-linked, online or other data-processing features. The Last updated date identifies the current policy version.

12. Contact

For privacy questions or requests about Wanted Streets: Open World Crime, email Caffeine Studios at thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com. Please include the game name in your message.
