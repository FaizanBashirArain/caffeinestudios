Trench Signal Privacy Policy

Trench Signal is a single-player game for Meta Quest developed by Caffeine Studios. This policy explains how the game handles your information and how to make a privacy request.

Effective November 12, 2026

Game data and saves

The game runs offline and does not require a Caffeine Studios account. It does not collect personal information for Caffeine Studios or use advertising, analytics, or online leaderboards.

The game saves campaign unlocks, best scores, medals, your Endless record, and whether you have seen the intro. It also saves preferences for audio, haptics, untimed play, and your preferred pointing hand. The game stores these on your headset to restore your progress and preferences. The game does not send these records to Caffeine Studios or other players. Caffeine Studios does not sell or share them.

Headset and controller input

The game uses headset and controller positions, orientations, and button inputs during play to render the scene, aim, and operate controls. The game does not record these inputs or transmit them off your headset. It does not access the camera, microphone, or location. Narration plays from prerecorded audio.

Meta services

Meta operates the headset, the Meta Horizon Store, account and purchase services, and Meta Cloud Backup. When Cloud Backup is active, Meta may back up eligible game saves. Meta handles information for these services under its own privacy policy.

Delete your data

You can remove local scores, progress, and preferences by clearing Trench Signal’s app data in your headset’s storage settings or uninstalling the game. If Meta Cloud Backup holds a copy, remove the game’s backups separately from your Meta Cloud Backups page. Caffeine Studios cannot access or delete data held only on your headset or in your Meta account.

To request deletion of information you sent to Caffeine Studios, email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com. Include the game name and identify the information you want deleted. There is no charge for making a request.

Support and privacy contact

If you contact us, we receive your email address and the details you choose to share. We use them to respond and handle your request. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. For privacy questions, email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com.
