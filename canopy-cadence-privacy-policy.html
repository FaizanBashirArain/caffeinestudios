Canopy Cadence Privacy Policy

Canopy Cadence is a single-player rhythm game for Meta Quest developed by Caffeine Studios. This policy explains what the game processes and stores and how you can delete that information.

Effective November 13, 2026

Game progress and settings

The game saves unlocked songs, best scores and medals, and whether you have seen the intro and finale. It also saves your difficulty, timing offset, audio levels, and haptics preference. It stores this information on your headset to restore your progress and settings. The local save stays on your headset until you clear the app’s data or uninstall it.

Canopy Cadence runs offline without a Caffeine Studios account. It has no advertising, analytics, multiplayer, or online leaderboards. The game does not send saves or gameplay information to Caffeine Studios or other players.

Headset and controller input

During play, the game uses the headset’s position and orientation to render your view. It uses controller positions, orientations, and button inputs to aim at menus, recognize taps and sweeps, and pause or recenter. The game processes these inputs in real time on your headset without saving or sending the raw input. The game does not access camera images, microphone audio, or geographic location. Narration uses prerecorded audio.

Meta services and cloud backups

Meta handles headset services, accounts, store purchases, and platform diagnostics under its own privacy policy. If Cloud Backup is enabled and the save is eligible, Meta may back up and restore your local game data. Caffeine Studios does not receive those backups.

Delete your data

To remove your local progress and settings, clear Canopy Cadence’s app data in your headset’s storage settings or uninstall the game. To delete any saved cloud copies, open Meta Cloud Backups and delete the Canopy Cadence backups for each headset you use.

To request deletion of personal information you sent to Caffeine Studios, email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com. Include the game name and identify the information you want deleted. We handle deletion requests at no charge. If we must retain information for legal reasons, we will explain why.

Support and privacy contact

If you email us, we receive your email address and the details you choose to send. We use them to respond and handle your request. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. For privacy questions, email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com.
