Cricket Chase Privacy Policy

Cricket Chase is a single-player cricket game for Meta Quest developed by Caffeine Studios. This policy explains how the game handles your information and how to make a privacy request.

Effective November 14, 2026

Match saves and settings

Cricket Chase stores unfinished T20 and ODI innings on your headset so you can resume play. Saves include each delivery’s outcome, runs, and the direction and distance of shots. The game clears the save when you finish the innings. Single Practice sessions have no save.

The game also stores your batting hand, gaze bowling preference, and audio levels on your headset so those settings carry over between sessions. Cricket Chase does not send match saves or preferences to Caffeine Studios. The game has no Caffeine Studios account, advertising, in-game analytics, or online leaderboards.

Headset and controller input

During play, the game uses headset and controller positions and orientations, along with controller buttons, to render your view, operate menus, and control the bat. Optional gaze bowling uses the direction your headset faces. Cricket Chase does not track your eyes. The game does not save raw headset or controller input or transmit it off your device.

Meta ownership checks and backup

When the Meta Horizon Store ownership check is enabled, the game uses the Meta Platform SDK to check whether your signed-in Meta account has access to Cricket Chase. The game uses the check result to decide whether to let you play. Cricket Chase does not request your Meta profile or send the ownership check result to Caffeine Studios.

Meta operates the headset, account, store, and Cloud Backup services. If you enable Cloud Backup, Meta may back up eligible app data. Meta handles information for these services under its privacy policy.

Delete your data

To remove match saves and preferences from your headset, clear Cricket Chase’s app data in storage settings or uninstall the game. If a cloud copy exists, delete any Cricket Chase backups for each device from your Meta Cloud Backups page. Caffeine Studios has no access to data held only on your headset or in your Meta account.

To request deletion of information you sent to Caffeine Studios, email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com. Include the game name and identify the information you want deleted.

Support and privacy contact

If you email Caffeine Studios, we receive your email address and the details you choose to share. We use this information to respond and handle your request. We keep correspondence as needed to resolve the request and meet legal obligations. We do not sell your information or share it for advertising. For privacy questions, email thecaffeinestudio@gmail.com.
