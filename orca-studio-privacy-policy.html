Orca Studio for iOS

Privacy Policy

Orca Studio is an augmented reality (AR) game. The app uses your camera for AR gameplay and does not collect personal information through the app.

Effective November 11, 2026

Camera access

With your permission, Orca Studio uses the live camera feed to display your surroundings and place virtual content in the real world. The app processes the feed on your device during gameplay. It does not save camera photos or videos, transmit the feed off your device, or share camera data with third parties.

You can deny camera access or revoke it at any time under Settings → Privacy & Security → Camera. The game’s AR features require camera access.

Data collection and retention

We do not collect personal information through Orca Studio. We have no personal information from the app to retain, sell, or share. For privacy questions or deletion requests, you can reach us at the email address below.
